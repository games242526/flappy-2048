How to Play

You are a 2048 tile in flight. Squeeze through the gaps in the green wall tiles — every wall you pass scores 1. Pass 5 walls and your tile merges up to the next number (2 → 4 → 8 …). Reach 2048 to win, then keep flying for an even higher score. Touch a wall and it's game over, and the walls get faster the longer you survive.

Controls

Tap anywhere, click, or press Space, ↑, or W to flap. Let go and you fall. New Game starts over, and your Best score is saved automatically.

Tips

Flaps are short hops, so tap in a steady rhythm instead of mashing, and aim for the middle of each gap. The floor and ceiling are safe — if the next gap is low, you can simply rest on the floor. The game pauses while this window is open or the page is hidden.
